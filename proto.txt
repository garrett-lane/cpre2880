Movement: 00
00 00 0000 forward
00 01 0000 backward
00 10 0000 left 
00 11 0000 right

Scan: 01
01 xxx xxx scan range
01 000 000 scan 0-90
01 111 000 scan 0-180
01 000 111 scan 90-90
01 011 011 scan 39-129
01 111 111 scan 90-180

Position: 10
10 0 00000 set x position on 6.7 cm intervals
10 10 0000 set angle (0, 90, 180, 270)
10 11 1000 negative x invert
10 11 0xxx add x centimeters to x position
10 11 1xxx subtract x centimeters to x position

Other: 11
11 000 000 ack
11 000 001 reset
